# MoneyMate Privacy Policy Draft

> This draft must be reviewed and completed with the legal entity, contact details, hosting regions and production vendors before publication.

MoneyMate processes account information, financial records entered by the user, receipt images, device/session identifiers, notification tokens and AI prompts needed to provide personal-finance features.

Data is used to authenticate users, synchronize their records, calculate reports and budgets, process receipts, deliver requested notifications, protect accounts and improve reliability. Data is not sold.

Receipt images and AI prompts may be sent to configured OCR or AI service providers. The production policy must name those providers, purposes, retention periods and international-transfer safeguards.

Users can delete their account from **Tài khoản → Xóa tài khoản**. Account deletion removes relational account data through database cascades. Production operations must also remove object-storage files, backups according to the retention schedule, notification tokens and processor-held data where applicable.

MoneyMate uses reasonable technical controls including encrypted transport, SecureStore/Keychain/Keystore for mobile credentials, hashed refresh tokens and access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

The final published policy must include controller identity, privacy contact, lawful bases, retention periods, user rights, complaint process, child-use restrictions and effective date.
